1.2 The Master Content State Machine

PLANNING → SCRIPT_WRITING → INTERNAL_SCRIPT_REVIEW → CLIENT_SCRIPT_REVIEW → READY_FOR_SHOOT / EDITING → INTERNAL_EDIT_REVIEW → CLIENT_REVIEW → FINAL_CLIENT_APPROVED → READY_TO_PUBLISH → PUBLISHED → REPORTING
💡
The Zero-Confusion Rule: If a button is not visible on your screen, it means either: (1) it is not your turn in the workflow, (2) the required predecessor file (like a Drive link or Script) has not been saved yet, or (3) your role lacks permission for that stage.

3. Script Writer Manual writer@agency.local

The Script Writer creates the narrative backbone. No video editing or shoot can occur until the script is written, reviewed, and approved.

Step-by-Step Writing & Submission Guide

Step 3.1: Locate Assigned Content Navigation

1. Sign in as writer@agency.local.

2. In the left sidebar, click Tasks to see SCRIPT · [Content Title] (Status: TO_DO), OR go to Content and click into the item card.

Step 3.2: Compose Script & Save Draft Drafting Form

Click on the horizontal tab: Script (between Overview and Tasks).

Field Label What to Type Purpose
The hook "Stop drinking 8 glasses of water all at once. Here's why." First 3 seconds scroll-stopping line.
Main script Full narration beat-by-beat, visual cues, speaker dialogue. The core video or copy content.
Call to action "Save this post and share with your workout buddy." Ending conversion prompt.
Caption Instagram caption copy formatted with line breaks. The final social post text.
Hashtags #MelloHealth #HydrationTips #Wellness Platform discovery tags.
Reference URLs Research links or competitor video URLs (1 per line). Creative references.

Click Action: Click the black button Save draft.

Output: Draft is safely persisted in the database. Status remains SCRIPT_WRITING. Team can review in-progress work without triggering false alerts.
Step 3.3: Submit Script for Review Clickable Action

1. Look at the very top-right header of the content detail page (next to the title).

2. Click the blue button: Submit script for review ↗.

3. In the popup modal, optionally type a handover comment (e.g. "Script ready for SMM review!").

4. Click Submit script for review.

Expected Output:
• Status transitions: SCRIPT_WRITING → INTERNAL_SCRIPT_REVIEW.
• Writer's task moves to FOR_REVIEW.
• SMM Review task is auto-generated for Aanya Sharma.
• Approvals menu on SMM sidebar immediately lights up with pulsing badge: • 1!

4. Videographer Manual shooter@agency.local

The Videographer manages on-set production when a content item has requiresShoot: true. This bridges the script and editing teams.

Step-by-Step Production Shoot Workflow

Step 4.1: Access & Edit Shoot Plan Shoot Tab

When the script is approved, status changes to READY_FOR_SHOOT.

1. Sign in as shooter@agency.local and open the content item.

2. Click on the Shoot tab. Fill out the on-set logistics form:

  • Date & time: Call time for camera and lighting.
  • Location: Studio, gym, client office, or outdoor set address.
  • Orientation: Portrait (9:16 for Reels/Shorts), Landscape (16:9), or Square (1:1).
  • Estimated duration: Production window in minutes (e.g. 120).
  • Shot list: Detailed checklist of angles, close-ups, and b-roll.
  • Props & People: Talent, models, product bottles, microphones needed.

Click Action: Click Save shoot plan.

Step 4.2: Confirm Shoot Schedule Top Header Action

1. In the top-right header, click: Confirm shoot schedule ↗.

2. Confirm the modal submission.

Output: Status transitions to SHOOT_SCHEDULED. Notifications are broadcast to SMM and client team.
Step 4.3: Complete Shoot & Upload Raw Footage Deliverable Hand-off

After wrapping production on set:

1. Upload raw camera clips and audio files to the client's Google Drive folder.

2. In the top-right header of the content page, click: Complete shoot ↗.

3. In the modal popup, enter:
• Raw footage Drive URL: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/... (Mandatory)
• Notes: e.g. "A-roll on Cam 1, high frame-rate b-roll on Cam 2."

4. Click Complete shoot.

Expected Output:
• Status transitions: SHOOT_SCHEDULED → SHOOT_COMPLETED → EDITING.
• Shoot task is marked COMPLETED.
• Raw footage link is auto-saved under the Drive links tab under category Raw Footage.
• Task EDIT · [Title] is auto-generated and assigned to the Video Editor (Arjun Patel)!

5. Video Editor Manual editor@agency.local

The Video Editor assembles the cut, audio, transitions, and on-screen text. In Agency OS, deliverables are managed through strict immutable versioning.

The Golden Rule of Version Submission

⚠️
Mandatory 2-Step Hand-Off: You CANNOT click "Submit edit" without first uploading a version link. If you attempt to submit without a version, the system will block you with: "Add a Google Drive version before submitting."

Step-by-Step Editing & Version Cut Guide

Step 5.1: Accept Editing Task Tasks Screen

1. Sign in as editor@agency.local.

2. Open Tasks in the sidebar. Click on EDIT · [Content Title].

3. Click Accept task (moves status to IN_PROGRESS).

4. Review the Script tab for captions/audio, and the Drive links tab for raw footage.

Step 5.2: Part A — Attach Version Cut (V1) Versions Tab Action

1. On the content detail page, click the horizontal tab: Versions.

2. In the top-right corner of the Versions panel, click the button: + Add version.

3. In the modal popup titled "Add the next version", fill in:

  • Google Drive URL: Direct link to the exported video file (e.g. https://drive.google.com/file/d/...).
  • What changed?: Editor notes (e.g. "Cut 1: High-energy pacing, animated captions, color graded.").

4. Click the modal button: Add version.

Output: Version record V1 is created with an Internal badge and an Open version ↗ link.
Step 5.3: Part B — Hand Off to SMM Review Top Header Action

1. Look at the top-right header bar of the content page (next to the main title).

2. You will now see the blue button: Submit edit for review ↗.

3. Click it, enter an optional note for your SMM, and click Submit edit for review.

Expected Output:
• Status transitions: EDITING → INTERNAL_EDIT_REVIEW.
• Review stage is set to SMM review.
• Editor's task updates to FOR_REVIEW.
• SMM Approvals radar lights up with • 1!

The SMM is the operational command center. SMM conducts internal reviews, approves creative cuts, schedules posts, copies captions, marks posts published, and tracks metrics.

6.1 The Approvals Radar & Review Actions

Screen: Left Sidebar → Approvals (/approvals) Review Hub

Whenever an approval requires your action, the Approvals link in the sidebar displays an animated glowing pulse dot: • 1.

1. Click Approvals. Filter by: All reviews, Internal review, or Client review.

2. Click on the content card to open review mode.

Header Buttons: Making the Review Decision Decision Actions
Button When to Click System Output
Request changes If script needs rewrite or video cut has audio/graphic flaws. Type feedback & timestamp notes (e.g. 00:14 Fix logo). Creates an official Revision round. Status moves to INTERNAL_CHANGES. Assigned employee task resets to CHANGES_REQUIRED.
Approve Work meets agency quality standards. Enter optional commendation note. Moves script/edit to Client Review (or next internal tier). Version tag changes to Shared with client. Client is notified.

6.2 The Publishing Hub & Metrics Form

Screen: Left Sidebar → Publishing (/publishing) Publishing Hub

Once client approves, status becomes FINAL_CLIENT_APPROVED. Item appears under Ready & scheduled.

Step 1: Mark Ready to Publish:
Open the content item. In the top-right header, click Mark ready to publish ↗. Status becomes READY_TO_PUBLISH.

Step 2: Copy Caption & Assets:
Click the Publishing tab. Click the button 📋 Copy caption & hashtags. Pre-formatted copy is instantly copied to your clipboard. Click Open in Drive ↗ to download the approved video cut.

Step 3: Publish to Platform & Record Live URL:
Upload the post live to Instagram/YouTube/LinkedIn. Return to Agency OS and click Mark published ↗ in the top header.
• Published post URL: Enter live post link (e.g. https://www.instagram.com/reel/...).
• External post ID: Platform ID (optional).
Click Mark published. Status moves to PUBLISHED!

Step 4: Record Post Analytics ("How did it do?"):
Under the Publishing tab, the Metrics Form unlocks. Enter:
Views, Likes, Comments, Shares, Saves, Reach, Impressions, Follower growth.
Click Save metrics. Data flows instantly into Reports and Client Portals!
